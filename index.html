Gravar Jr. Help

v1.5

Purpose: Gravar Jr. is a simplified clinical video observation notebook. It is intended for watching video and creating time-linked notes, lightweight transcription rows, and codes.

Basic workflow

  • Load a local video file, a YouTube URL, or a direct browser-playable video URL.
  • Pause at a clinically meaningful moment and choose Use current time.
  • Add a note, transcript, or code. End time is optional.
  • Click Replay to play a row; click the row body to load it for editing. Add annotation always appends a new row.
  • Save a project JSON to keep editing later. Export CSV or HTML for sharing/review.

Streaming limits

YouTube playback uses the official embedded player. Direct URLs work only when the site permits browser playback and embedding. Some institutional or commercial sites may block access, timing, or embedding.

Not included in v1.0

No ELAN functions, waveform analysis, pop-out windows, dual-video synchronization, or instruction/student mode.
